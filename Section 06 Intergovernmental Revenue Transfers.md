
# Section 06: Intergovernmental Transfer Revenues 

Note: These section guides do not show every question, but highlight specific, complicated questions. 

 

_**Important:**_ 

_“Your tribal government and its service entities” refers to your tribal government itself and any of its legally separate governmental service-providing entities or component units, such as authorities for tribal housing, education, utilities, or health. In this section, do not include any information about enterprises owned by your tribal government that are primarily intended to be profit-generating businesses, such as gaming operations – except transfers and distributions from these businesses to your tribal government and its service agencies._  


_While your tribal government is free to decline to answer any question, providing numeric responses wherever possible will result in reporting that is far more accurate and useful for your tribal government. If you are unsure of an exact amount, an estimate is greatly preferable over leaving an item blank. When entering numbers, enter 0 only if an amount was actually zero. Do not add symbols like dollar signs, negative signs, commas, or periods._

## What to Include and Exclude 
In the survey fiscal year, what was the amount of intergovernmental revenues—other than taxes—received by your tribal government and its service entities from other governments?  

For all amounts in this section, include:  
-  Grants  
-  Payments in lieu of taxes (PILT)  
-  Reimbursements for services performed for other governments  
-  Payments under the American Recovery and Reinvestment Act of 2009 (ARRA), the American Rescue Plan Act of 2021 (ARPA), and other similar structures  
-  P.L. 93-638 contract/compact revenues  

For all amounts in this section, exclude:  
-  Loans  
-  Revenues collected through intergovernmental tax agreements (report these in the Tax Revenues section that will come later)  
-  Receipts from utility sales to other governments (report these in the Miscellaneous Revenues section that will come later)

### Why do we ask this series of questions?  

The Center for Indian Country Development (CICD) is interested in this series of questions because it helps illustrate how much revenue tribal governments are receiving from other governments. Once this data is analyzed, the information may provide your tribal nation about comparable distributions.  

 

### What should be included in this section?  

In this section, please include anything that falls in the above amounts. This includes revenue that comes from intergovernmental aid agreements.  

 

As you include grants, please include competitive grants, as well as legislative appropriations that come to tribal governments that may or may not be a grant and proceeds from other bonding activity. Grants may include but are not limited to federal Climate Pollution Reduction grants, Indian Housing Block Grant, etc.  

 

Here in this section, please also include housing choice vouchers, New Market Tax Credits and Low-Income Housing Tax Credits.  

 

Additionally, please include a loan that has been forgiven.  

 

### What should be excluded from your answers in this section?  

Please exclude current forgivable or deferred loans. Once a loan is forgiven, the amount that has been forgiven should be included in this section.  

 
## Question 1 Total revenue received from local governments

### Question Text:

From local governments (e.g., counties and cities)  

How much was received directly from these governments? {Numeric, USD} 

 

### Why do we ask this question?  

The answers to this question will help the Center for Indian Country Development (CICD) provide comparisons to local governments.  

  

### What should be included in this question? 

In addition to include revenues received from county and city governments, please also include revenues from metropolitan planning organizations, regional councils, watershed districts, etc.  

 

## Question 2 Total revenue from temporary local government funding programs 

### Question Text:

Of this total, how much came from temporary programs from which you do not anticipate recurring or future revenues (e.g., COVID-19 relief)? Please enter 0 if the temporary or one-time amount was zero. {Numeric, USD} 

 

### Why do we ask this question?  

The Center for Indian Country Development (CICD) asks this question to help provide comparisons to local, state, and federal governments, when the data is accessible.   

 

### What should be included in this question? 

This section should include both one-time funding and recurring/future revenues that are time limited.  

 

Similar to question 1, please also include revenues received from county and city governments, metropolitan planning organizations, regional councils, watershed districts, etc.
