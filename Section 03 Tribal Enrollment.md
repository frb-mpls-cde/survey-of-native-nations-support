# Section 03: Tribal Enrollment 

Note: These section guides do not show every question, but highlight specific, complicated questions. 

**_Important:_** 

_“Your tribal government and its service entities” refers to your tribal government itself and any of its legally separate governmental service-providing entities or component units, such as authorities for tribal housing, education, utilities, or health. In this section, do not include any information about enterprises owned by your tribal government that are primarily intended to be profit-generating businesses, such as gaming operations – except transfers and distributions from these businesses to your tribal government and its service agencies._  

_While your tribal government is free to decline to answer any question, providing numeric responses wherever possible will result in reporting that is far more accurate and useful for your tribal government. If you are unsure of an exact amount, an estimate is greatly preferable over leaving an item blank. When entering numbers, enter 0 only if an amount was actually zero. Do not add symbols like dollar signs, negative signs, commas, or periods._

## Question 1 Tribe's Total Enrollment

### Question text:

Enrollment data allow for comparative analysis of a tribe’s revenues and expenditures. In addition to fiscal analysis, enrollment data are critical for assessing how tribal governments provide essential services to their citizens. 


What was your tribe’s total enrollment as of March 12, 2022? An estimate is acceptable. 


Below, please share any additional information related to your tribe’s enrollment. 


### Why do we ask this question? 

Providing enrollment data will allow us to provide per capita financial information as well as to do comparative analysis of your tribe to the aggregate (summarized data) of tribes of a similar size. We also recognize that each tribal government has different enrollment criteria. Any additional information you can share can assist us in providing you with the best possible analysis.
